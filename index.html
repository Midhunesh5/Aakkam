The Sacred Dance of Heritage
& Innovation

Anchoring Craft Education / Craft Pathways / Aakkam’s Network of Craft Practices Aakkam works with craftspeople and learners across Bhavani, Samathur, Thanjavur and Gandhigram. The school creates spaces for weaving practice, craft learning and cultural inquiry. Through meetings, workshops, product development sessions and focused displays, Aakkam enables makers, students and visitors to learn from each other and build a deeper understanding of these living traditions.

The Soul of Aakkam

The Vision

To reimagine the future of India’s arts and crafts through collaboration, creativity, and care. Rooted in communities yet open to the world, the initiative will engage in creative documentation, education, and co-creation — enabling traditions to evolve, adapt, and thrive across generations. It seeks to find ways for crafts and culture to meaningfully respond to the needs and aspirations of the present time.

Our Pursuit

Reimagine the way crafts are seen through collaboration and co-creation.

Bring together different disciplines to spark innovation in culture and design.

Create learning spaces that inspire, connect communities, and nurture creativity.

Build meaningful and inclusive work opportunities in the creative sector.

Our Spirit

As patrons of our fine arts, religion, philosophy, and literature, we should try to promote and preserve our traditional arts. Our rich cultural heritage gives us great joy and satisfaction especially when our arts have bakthi in their theme. For hundreds of years our artists have practised all forms of art, to educate and entertain people, thereby contributing to the healthy living both in mind and body. Art, music, and literature cannot be separated, they are part of us. A society is judged by its cultural traditions and life is a reflection and an expression of different art forms.

- Arucchelvar Dr. Mahalingam Ayya
